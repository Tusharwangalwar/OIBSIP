01 / THE MAN BEHIND THE VISION

A life shaped by purpose.

“The best brains of the nation may be found on the last benches of the classroom.”

— Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Raised in a modest family, he developed an early interest in learning, mathematics and flight.

He studied aerospace engineering and went on to make major contributions to India's space and defence programmes. His work with India's space and missile projects earned him the popular title “Missile Man of India.”

In 2002, Kalam became the 11th President of India. He connected especially with young people, visiting schools and colleges to encourage scientific curiosity, confidence and service to the nation.

Remembered as a scientist, author and dedicated teacher, he passed away on 27 July 2015 while delivering a lecture to students. His message of learning and dreaming continues to inspire.
